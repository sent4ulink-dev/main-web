PETAL ISLE · A GARDEN ABOVE THE CLOUDS

A little magic, made with love.

The floating garden is the Petal Isle refined Blender scene supplied by the project’s owner. The island, the gardens, the stone with the question, the adventure board, the calendar tree, the map, the keepsake book and the camera composition are drawn with Three.js from an optimised glTF export.

The pictures of a fairy facing the camera were supplied by the project’s owner and adjusted into wingless characters. Wingless male characters were created to match the second model you can choose. Their leaf tags, glow, fairy dust and animated translucent wings are assembled and placed in Three.js.

The earlier procedural test version used the work “Lunox - Butterfly Seraphim” (creator: Maxell A.; licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International) for its shapes and proportions.

That model and its animation are kept in the project out of respect for their source, but the current page does not load them.

The earlier night-forest version used the work “Billions stars Skybox HDRI panorama” (creator: Aliaksandr.melas; licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International) for its sky. The embedded panorama was extracted from the supplied GLB without any loss of quality and is kept in the project out of respect for its source. The current page uses a procedural daytime blue sky, so it does not load this panorama.
