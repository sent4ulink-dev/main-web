Love LCD — Mongolian pixel font for this invitation.
Original 70 Cyrillic glyphs, including Өө and Үү, drawn on a pixel grid inspired
by the user's reference sheet. This is not an exact extraction of the image.
Latin, digits and symbols derive from VT323 by Peter Hull under SIL OFL 1.1.
The derivative is renamed Love LCD and remains under the included OFL.txt.
Rebuild: python scripts/build_pixel_font.py (fonttools, brotli).
