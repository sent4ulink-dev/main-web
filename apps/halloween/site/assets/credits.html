A little art, a lot of thanks.

Animated black cat

The cat animation was supplied for this invitation. It was isolated from the original video, with the window and desk removed, and converted into a transparent looping animation. Its original appearance and movement are preserved. A still version is provided for reduced motion.

Cartoon night scenery

An original illustration generated for this invitation, with purple hills, crooked trees, warm lanterns, and cheerful pumpkins.

A little heart ghost

An original heart-shaped ghost illustration generated for this invitation. Its happy star eyes and rosy cheeks join the love-letter finale, with a gentle animated peek and a still appearance for reduced motion.

A little love letter

Love letter (U+1F48C) and sparkling heart (U+1F496) by Google’s Animated Noto Emoji, licensed under CC BY 4.0. The animation files and still SVGs from fonts.gstatic.com are preserved; the site adds gentle floating motion around them.

Jumpscare photo and sound

The jumpscare photo was supplied for this invitation. Jumpscare Scream by Breviceps is released under CC0. Playback uses a short excerpt at reduced volume. Button plops and magical chimes are synthesized locally for this site.
