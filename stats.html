Visits are counted on sent4u.link’s own server without cookies, so ad blockers don’t hide anyone. A visitor is counted once per day. Your own visits aren’t counted while you’re signed in. Visits from data centres, clouds and VPN networks are counted apart as probably automated: scanners, crawlers that pretend to be a browser, and people on a VPN.
